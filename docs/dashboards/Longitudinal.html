Longitudinal

Per wave, all sociodemographic groups with less than 40 participants have been removed (for the sake of weightening). Most of the times, this is Brussels and Wallonia in wave 1 and 2 as, then, the project was only targeting Flemish participants. From Survey 3 on, we included Belgium in total. The specific information is provided in tab ‘Sociodemographics’

Participants are included with at least having participated -4 the total number of waves
